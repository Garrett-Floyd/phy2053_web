Theorem 18.2.4.

The average speed of an object undergoing circular motion is
\begin{equation*} s_{avg} = r \omega_{z,avg} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.

From Definition 3.1.2 and equation (18.1.1) we have
\begin{equation*} s_{avg} = \frac{d}{\Delta t} = \frac{r \Delta \theta_{z}}{\Delta t}. \end{equation*}
Then by Definition 18.1.3 we have
\begin{equation*} s_{avg} = r \omega_{z,avg}. \end{equation*}
