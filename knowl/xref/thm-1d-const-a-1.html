Theorem 4.1.1.

When the x-component of the acceleration is constant
\begin{equation*} v_{x, f} = a_{x} \Delta t + v_{x, i}. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.

If the x-component of acceleration is constant then by the definition of average acceleration (Definition 3.1.3) we have
\begin{equation*} a_{x} = a_{avg,x} = \frac{v_{x,f} - v_{x,i}}{\Delta t}. \end{equation*}
Rearranging, we get
\begin{align*} v_{x,f} - v_{x,i} \amp = a_{x} \Delta t \\ \amp\\ v_{x,f} \amp = a_{x} \Delta t + v_{x,i} \end{align*}
